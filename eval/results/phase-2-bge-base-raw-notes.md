# Phase 2 — raw-query retrieval: where it fails, and what the scores look like

Qualitative notes, not an eval run: there is no golden set yet, so nothing
here is a recall number. This is the "before" picture the golden set gets
seeded from, and the evidence `config.MIN_SCORE_THRESHOLD` will be tuned
against in Phase 4.

| | |
|---|---|
| embedder | `bge-base` = `BAAI/bge-base-en-v1.5`, 768-dim, query instruction on queries only |
| strategy | `raw` (identity) |
| router | `keyword` |
| index | 487 × 768 float32, exact cosine |
| sentence-transformers / torch / numpy | 6.0.1 / 2.14.0+cu130 / 2.5.3 |
| python | 3.14.4 |
| date | 2026-09-08 |

Thirty probe queries: the 14 `in_scope` and 6 `out_of_scope` entries of
`eval/router_set.jsonl`, six more in-scope ones written for coverage, three
lexically anchored canaries, and one more plainly out-of-scope. All were run
through `index.vector_index.search` with `k=5`; the keyword router routes
every one of them `IN_SCOPE`, so what follows is what the pipeline would
show. Full top-5 lists are at the end.

## 1. The score distribution

The plan predicted 0.6–0.75 for unrelated pairs. That is not what bge-base
does once the query instruction is applied.

| population | n | top-1 range | top-1 median |
|---|---|---|---|
| in-scope, modern phrasing | 20 | 0.459 – 0.599 | 0.55 |
| lexically anchored canaries | 3 | 0.643 – 0.652 | 0.65 |
| plainly out of scope | 7 | 0.389 – 0.562 | 0.42 |

Corpus-wide medians (the score of the *typical* passage against a query)
sit at 0.27–0.46, and the 90th percentile at 0.33–0.52. So the space is
anisotropic — nothing is near zero — but less so than assumed.

What matters is the overlap, and it is real:

| out-of-scope query | top-1 | beats how many in-scope top-1s |
|---|---|---|
| what were the causes of the Second Punic War | 0.562 | 12 of 20 |
| which tax software should I use | 0.521 | 6 of 20 |
| summarize the plot of Hamlet | 0.517 | 6 of 20 |
| what's the weather in Zurich tomorrow | 0.425 | 0 |
| write me a python function to reverse a linked list | 0.419 | 0 |
| best pizza toppings | 0.415 | 0 |
| how do I fix a leaking radiator valve | 0.389 | 0 |

The six in-scope queries below 0.49 are the conceptual-gap ones: "everyone
online seems to be doing better than me" (0.459), "I'm jealous of my
friend's success" (0.472), "I can't sleep because I'm dreading Monday"
(0.475), "I got passed over for promotion again" (0.476), "I'm anxious about
a presentation tomorrow" (0.488), "my coworker takes credit for my work"
(0.491). These are exactly the queries the project exists to serve, and a
threshold that rejected the Punic War would reject all of them.

**Consequences for `MIN_SCORE_THRESHOLD`:**

- It can be a conservative floor, around **0.40**: that rejects the
  radiator, the pizza and the linked list, and nothing in-scope, with the
  lowest in-scope top-1 at 0.459 leaving ~0.06 of margin.
- It cannot be an out-of-scope detector. Anything above ~0.46 starts costing
  in-scope queries before it catches the tax software, Hamlet or the Punic
  War. Out-of-scope detection is the router's job — this is the Phase 4
  LLM-router case stated as a number rather than as an assertion.
- The canaries live in their own band (0.64–0.65), above every other top-1
  seen. Worth remembering when a canary "passes" at 0.50 — something moved.
- The margin between top-1 and the corpus median is not a better
  discriminator: the tax-software query has the *widest* margin in the set
  (0.52 over a 0.27 median) because 4.13 happens to say "why then do you
  not use it?".

## 2. Where raw-query retrieval fails

In rough order of how often it showed up.

**Lexical hijack.** One shared word outranks the concept.

| query | rank-1 hit | the word |
|---|---|---|
| I'm anxious about a presentation tomorrow | 4.47 "you must die tomorrow" | *tomorrow* |
| I can't sleep because I'm dreading Monday | 8.12 "reluctant to be roused from sleep" | *sleep* |
| my coworker takes credit for my work | 6.42 "co-operating in one great work" | *work* |
| which tax software should I use | 4.13 "why then do you not use it?" | *use* |
| I said something cruel and can't take it back | 6.27 "is it not cruel to restrain men" | *cruel* |
| how do I stop procrastinating | 2.4 "think of your long procrastination" | *procrastination* — a hit, but for the wrong reason |

In three of these the apt passage is in the list one or two ranks down
(7.8 "Be not troubled about the future" at 2; 5.25 / 7.26 at 2–3 for the
coworker; 7.27 at 1 for jealousy). Rerank should recover those; rewriting
should stop the hijack at source.

**The conceptual gap.** No shared vocabulary, and nothing apt at rank 1.

- "I got passed over for promotion again" → 5.6 (on reckoning up favours),
  4.14, 7.2, 8.5, 9.2. None speak to ambition or external goods.
- "everyone online seems to be doing better than me" → 9.23, 9.19, 9.11
  before 12.4 at rank 5, which is the one that fits ("every man loves
  himself beyond all others, yet holds his own opinion of himself in less
  regard than that of others").
- "I feel like nothing I do matters" → 6.32, 4.24, 7.20: adjacent but not
  on point.

**Book I noise.** "I'm jealous of my friend's success" → 1.14 at rank 3
("Of Severus, my brother, I learned…"). One observed case; the Phase 4
`book == 1` filter experiment has its first example.

**A pull toward one-sentence passages.** 12.25 ("Cast away opinion, and
you are saved.") appears at rank 3 for the lost argument and rank 5 for the
cruel remark; 11.30 ("Yourself a slave, your speech cannot be free.") tops
the cruel-remark query at 0.549. Short passages seem to sit closer to
short queries. Not measured — worth checking whether length correlates
with score across the corpus before deciding it needs handling.

**Register and entity similarity.** "What were the causes of the Second
Punic War" → 3.3 (Hippocrates, the Chaldeans), 8.3 (Alexander, Caesar,
Pompey), 8.31 (the court of Augustus) at 0.50–0.56. The embedder is matching
Roman names and a historical register, not the question. Nothing
post-retrieval fixes this; it is a routing failure.

**Where it works.** Death (8.58 at 0.583 / 0.650), anger (10.30, 5.28),
fame (4.19 at 0.60 / 0.65), pain (7.64, 8.28, 8.40, 7.33 — four apt
passages in the top five), the opinions of others (2.15, 5.3, 7.44), rude
people (5.28, 11.9, 10.30, 9.42, 7.26), what is in your control (8.36,
8.29). Where the corpus has the vocabulary, raw retrieval is fine — which is
what Risk 7 in the plan predicted, and what makes the hard set matter.

## 3. Safety interactions observed

- **Grief and the death-counsel list.** "my father died last month and I
  can't function" fires `MENTAL_HEALTH` on *can't function*; its rank-1 hit
  is 8.47 (0.551), which is on the death-counsel list, so it is withheld and
  four passages are shown with a note. 8.47 opens with the best grief
  passage in the book ("it is not the thing itself which afflicts you, but
  your judgment") and ends "Quit life then, in the same kindly spirit" —
  which is why it is on the list, and which a bereaved reader should not be
  handed. The outcome is right and the cost is the query's best hit. Carry
  this case in `safety_set.jsonl` so the trade stays visible; it is also the
  strongest argument for Phase 4's sub-passage handling being limited to
  11.18 — 8.47 has no seam either.
- **Over-firing, as designed.** "this weather is depressing" and "my
  therapist says I should journal" both raise `MENTAL_HEALTH`; "I lose my
  temper with my kids and hate myself after" raises it on *hate myself*.
  Each costs one referral paragraph. Acceptable per the plan; the Phase 3
  false-positive rate will say whether the list needs pruning.
- **A recall miss, fixed.** "my manager humiliates me in front of the team
  every day and HR does nothing" raised no flag on first run: nothing in
  the abuse list matched *humiliate*. The list now covers workplace and
  social mistreatment that never says "abuse" (humiliate, belittle, demean,
  picked on, singled out, frozen out, screams at me, …). The Phase 3 safety
  set should carry several of these phrasings.
- **Suppression is gated, and it shows.** "my coworker takes credit for my
  work" surfaces 7.26 at rank 3 with no flag, and 7.26 is shown. The same
  passage under an `ABUSE` flag would be withheld. That is the design: the
  hazard is a property of the situation, not the passage.

## 4. Seeds for the golden set

Hard, from the failures above: promotion, social comparison, jealousy,
dreading Monday, the presentation, the coworker, the cruel remark, nothing
matters. Canaries: fearing death (8.58), anger (10.30), fame (4.19), pain
(7.64). Out-of-scope hard cases for the router set: the Punic War already
in the set is the best one — it is the query the embedder is most confident
about and most wrong.
